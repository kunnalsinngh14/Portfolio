A premium, motion-driven portfolio website showcasing my journey as a B.Tech Computer Science (AI/ML) student at RV University. This project highlights my expertise in Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, and Creative Development through a cinematic, interactive 3D user interface.

---

## ✨ Features

## Cinematic Splash Screen: A two-stage loading sequence featuring identity scaling and a cubic-bezier vertical split reveal.

## Interactive 3D Background: Built with Three.js (r134) featuring 250 emerald-green floating particles with mouse parallax and additive blending.

## Dynamic Theme Engine: Seamlessly switch between a deep slate-black aesthetic and light mode, with persistent localStorage state.

## Advanced Animations: Coordinated scroll-reveal effects powered by GSAP and ScrollTrigger, paired with Vanilla-Tilt.js for 3D perspective on glass cards.

## Typewriter Role Cycling: A character-by-character rendering effect showcasing roles: AI/ML Enthusiast, Software Developer, and Data Science Explorer.

### Sectional Design:

- **About:** Personal bio integrated with high-impact stat pills (CGPA, 5+ Projects).

- **Skills:** Categorized pill badges for Languages, AI/ML Frameworks, and DevOps tools.

- **Projects:** Interactive showcase of 4 featured works including Snake Game and RoadLens (CNN).

- **Certifications:** Verifiable achievement rows with direct Google Drive links.
---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

**Core Graphics:** Utilizes Three.js for a fixed-canvas background to ensure zero layout interference.

**Styling & UI:** Leverages Tailwind CSS (CDN JIT) for utility-first design with a custom Black & Emerald Green color system.

**Motion Engine:** Uses GSAP 3.12.2 for premium easing and ScrollTrigger for coordinated section reveals.

**Interactivity:** Integrated Vanilla-Tilt 1.8.0 for micro-interactions and FontAwesome 6.4 for scalable vector iconography.

**Typography:** A professional trio of Archivo Black (Splash), Space Grotesk (Display), and Inter (Body) via Google Fonts.
